## Overview
ACM is organized to serve students for academic, professional, and social
purposes at TAMUSA. The Chapter shall be involved with and conduct activities that include:

- Engaging students in stimulating computing activities
- Connecting students with leaders in the computing field
- Encouraging the pursuit of career opportunities in computing
- Mentoring and tutoring students for academic and professional success
- Broadening both the University and local K-12 STEM communities through ACM
- Building a network of students, to include current, former, and future; businesses; and local leaders for the benefit of ACM members
