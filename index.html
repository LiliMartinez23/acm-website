Student Chapter

ACM Student Chapter is organized to serve students for academic, professional, and social purposes at Texas A&M University-San Antonio.

About ACM Student Chapter

By creating a community among students within the Computing and Cyber Security Department and anyone else interested in technology, we welcome all students interested in solving real-world problems using applied computing skills.

Sub Organizations

ACM-W

ACM-W is our sister organization dedicated to supporting and empowering women in computing. Through mentorship, networking, and community-building, ACM-W fosters an inclusive environment where all identities are valued and encouraged to thrive. By uplifting diverse voices, we aim to make technology a field where everyone belongs.
